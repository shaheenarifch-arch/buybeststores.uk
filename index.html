Answers for buyers and sellers

Frequently asked questions

Can I purchase phones directly from SmatTech?

Verified SmatTech-owned stock will use an on-site basket and server-verified Stripe or PayPal checkout. Until real prices and inventory are loaded, listed phone families use early-access registration rather than simulated purchase data.

How will UK delivery work?

Eligible SmatTech-owned products will normally offer tracked delivery within 2 to 7 working days. Same-day and next-day options will depend on postcode, stock, cutoff time and confirmed payment.

How is a device valuation confirmed?

The initial estimate is reviewed by an administrator. Final offers require ownership, IMEI, activation-lock, physical-condition and functional checks.

Who handles fitness-product delivery?

Fitness products accessed through partner links are sold and fulfilled by the external retailer. The retailer manages payment, delivery, cancellation, returns and warranty support.
