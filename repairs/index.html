Phone and Tablet Repair Assessment

SmartTech provides a structured route for customers to request assessment of phone and tablet faults including damaged screens, weak batteries, charging problems, camera faults, speaker or microphone issues, software problems and water-damage concerns. The information and photographs supplied before inspection help the team understand the reported problem, but a remote estimate is not a final diagnosis. No additional chargeable work should begin without customer approval of the scope and price.

How the service works

  1. Select the device and describe the reported fault.
  2. Upload photographs of the device and visible damage.
  3. Choose an available mail-in, collection or drop-off route.
  4. Receive a diagnostic assessment or quotation.
  5. Approve or decline the proposed work before chargeable repair begins.
  6. After repair, the device is functionally checked and prepared for return.
  7. Keep the repair reference and warranty details for future support.

Data and repair preparation

Customers should back up data before service and remove personal information where practical. Some repairs require device testing, software restoration or access to basic functions. SmartTech will not intentionally access unrelated personal content. Data loss can occur during hardware or software service, particularly with water damage or storage faults, so a current backup is important. A repair warranty applies only to the stated part and workmanship and does not erase applicable statutory rights.
